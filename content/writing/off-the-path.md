if most answer yes he’ll likely say no
then smile and wink to subtly show
the answer is not always found in the crowd.
sometimes the mass completely enshrouds
what’s hidden to all, but a few trav’ling seekers,
because it wasn’t played on Jerusalem’s speakers. 
–
his job is to teach but that wasn’t his plan
and his vow against such was likely in crayon. 
often you’ll think he must be a child
as his laughter erupts after pants are defiled.
finding his peace he’ll finish the speech,
“the kingdom’s for kids. so don’t overreach.”
–
actions and words work to echo each other. 
a pattern he learned from his older brother. 
he’ll give you time he doesn’t even have
for crying out loud this man’s also a dad
to six littles who may or may not see
that their life never is nor will be free.
–
but neither was his, or yours, or mine
without Him as payment we’d still be with swine
and searching for words to simply convey
it’s wasted and gone, “i have nothing to pay.”
but He’s not after money all He wants is your heart
“love God and love people” that’s the end and the start
–
they share the same message, he and his Brother. 
smile, listen, love and be nice to each other.
in each of their stories it’s rarely in crowds
when help from above is fully endowed.
follow the angels who endlessly sing
to find your Redeemer, Savior, and King.
–
that’s how i found Him— off the path in a manger. 
introduced as old friends, no longer as strangers.
