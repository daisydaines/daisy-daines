Last night I participated in a 'Payments Strategy' meeting with Paxton, Cam W. and Blakely. All three are insanely impressively dynamic, high-agency, strategically minded individuals. 

It's obvious as soon as a meeting or brainstorm session starts that they are high caliber dudes. Their intellectual horsepower is high. 

They break problems down simply. They see all the factors concurrently. 

Unit economics,
product details, 
competitive landscape,
strategic implications, 
and all the other things that are beyond my business brain. 

They bounce ideas, takes, questions and answers off of each other back and forth like an Olympic game of ping pong. One after the other. 

Ping. Pong. Ping. Pong. 

Being in the room with them is equally as impressive as it is exhausting. Not because of them or their tactics, but because of me and my apathy. 

I sit within arms length of a strategic masterclass yet my gaze drifts out the window and my thoughts float even further. 

I try to stay engaged. I try to follow the intensity and match the energy, but I struggle. 

I'm trying to figure out why. Part of me feels like I've decided this doesn't matter as much as everyone acts like it does. At least that's what I keep telling myself. 

Prior to the meeting Blakely briskly walked past my desk and saw me working on a model for Cam's new loyalty product. 

"I see you are working on that loyalty analysis. How's it coming?"

    "Figuring it out so far" (most my answers are so vague because of my lack of interest)

"Good. I'm glad."

    "Yup."

"You've been doing great work. Hoping we can get you on some more analyses. Aren't you loving it?"

    "ha.. yupp.."

"Nice!" (as he continued to buzz off to another meeting.)

tbh, most of it is meh. that's literally the best word I can come up with the describe the feeling I have most of my days at Redo lately. 

Is it lack of purpose in this work? That combined with the grief that I feel regularly? maybe...

I'm just not sure what the solution is and I'm a problem solver so it bugs me to feel stuck.
