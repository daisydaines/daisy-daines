His birth and life had been foretold 
and prayed for by both young and old. 
This {#fcbb00cc:King} and {#fcbb00cc:Lion} would soon deliver
from Empires that were always bigger!
.
The {#fcbb00cc:Victorious One} was born in a barn
maybe to show He meant no harm.
No! Soon, He’d build his powerful battalion
and conquer with sword atop magnificent stallion! 
.
as a boy He carpentered under his Father 
shaped and sanded and made much stronger.
Perfect! He knows how to work with his hands
He’ll lead, as {#fcbb00cc:Almighty}, into foreign lands! 
.
Building the Kingdom seemed to dawn slowly. 
He introduced beggar, sick, lost, and lowly
in heart to His castle that He’d yet to start laying
the revolution, it seemed, He was only delaying.
.
How were we ever to win this great fight? 
without sword, or dominion, and only two mites!
was {#fcbb00cc:Jesus} to be our conquering {#fcbb00cc:Brother}?
what has He done? do we look for another? 
.
He called evil spirits out from her head
and commanded the lame to take up his bed.
He declared, to the woman, that she was set free
from the prison of guilt to which He holds the key.
.
to those who were blind He quickly gave sight
to others in darkness He is {#fcbb00cc:Everlasting Light}.
to those who’ve felt lost for too many days
have peace and know He is also {#fcbb00cc:The Way}. 
.
When doubt and loss crawl into your life
And the pit to escape is too great a height
Or you feel you’ve fallen from Father’s favor
Remember God’s love is His {#fcbb00cc:Mediator}.
.
He wasn’t sent to kill or raise the sword
or start fights or battles, but to end the War.
On errand of His Father, the {#fcbb00cc:Shepherd} brought cheese
to fight the war waged by a giant who breathes,
.
         “Lost.           Dead.        Gone.       Worthless.     
Empty.    Forgotten.    Guilty.     Hopeless.”
Is there not a cause to defeat this enemy?
whose taunts compete for our identity!
.
{#fcbb00cc:Christ} lives and died as our {#fcbb00cc:Infinite Hope} 
to pay our staggering debt because we’re all broke.
You quietly question, “for a person like me?”
God speaks, “You’re Mine,” based on pedigree.
.
Between two thieves, on the middle tree
The {#fcbb00cc:Lamb of God} suffered willingly.
And crowned with thorns the {#fcbb00cc:King} decreed,
Whom the {#fcbb00cc:Son} sets free is free indeed! 
.
so when your journey pit stops at Bethesda’s pool,
and this season of life feels more cruel,
alone and afterthought are not your story’s bells
because forever His name is {#fcbb00cc:Emmanuel}.
