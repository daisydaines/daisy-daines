was He betrayed with a kiss?
or with a heart lost in greed? 
we say we’ll never leave Jesus
yet we’ve denied the Nazarene.
so before you grab your sword
and decide to fight your way out
the price isn’t just thirty pieces
it’s His life we’re talking about. 

He lost in a fixed court, 
was less popular than a thug,
without even saying a word
His grave was already dug. 
they nailed Him to a cross.
His charge? King of the Jews
fraud, fake, and fool they said
but He was wearing our shoes.
