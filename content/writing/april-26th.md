last night was scary. 

remi was going through it all day. complaining about her stomach and her head. crying over the pain she was in. she was heating up most of the day and grace decided to check her temperature after dinner. 102*. yikes. 

we gave her some tylenol and she was much better for bedtime. 

a few hours later she woke up crying. we rushed in and she was soaked. just laying in a pool of sweat. 

grace changed her clothes, held her and gave her more medicine. she slowly calmed and was ready to lay down again. 

meanwhile, I was in and out of her room. I had the thermometer in one hand and medication in the other. it was dark and we were huddled by her crib. 

my mind flashed back to dozens of middle of the night huddles in the same spot with Esther. 

I had to leave Remi's room twice because it was starting to bring back painful feelings and scare me. 

How was she sick with a fever and struggling to sleep exactly two years after Esther? 

How was this happening again? and why? 

I cried in grace's arms before we shuffled back to bed. it just felt like too much. 

after a long stormy sunday the lyrics from Flowers by Samantha Ebert and Seph Schleuter

"And I'll thank you for the rain, the hurt and days of pain"
