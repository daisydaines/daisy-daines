*A poem written for Big Kev. Father's Day 2019*
______

amidst the snores and tight closed eyes
the soldier wakes and kneels to rise
he meets the day while still is won
the darkness before the rising sun.

–

he’s off to fight and battle for
his army of years six and score.
his heart is steady and beats convey
devotion to his Commander’s way.

–

when war wages and is too hard to fight
when darkness roars and clouds the light
i see the soldier kneels again
and seals his plea with a believing “Amen”

–

he teaches that true strength is found
in war torn knees that meet the ground.
though his position and path may alter
his Champion will never falter.

–

my time to fight comes soon enough
and when it comes i pray i’m tough
and strong and steady and never cease
to find the power that comes from Peace.

–

a fighting soldier i’m trying to be
but from my view begin to see
i’ll always be the admiring lad
who calls the fighting soldier dad.
