This has been an interesting week feeling a pull to participate less in the way of the world than I already do. 

Reading in 1 Samuel about Saul and his actions that drew him away from God. 

    22 And Samuel said, Hath the Lord as great delight in burnt offerings and sacrifices, as in obeying the voice of the Lord? 
    Behold, to obey is better than sacrifice, and to hearken than the fat of rams.

    23 For rebellion is as the sin of witchcraft, and stubbornness is as iniquity and idolatry. 
    Because thou hast rejected the word of the Lord, he hath also rejected thee from being king.

    24 And Saul said unto Samuel, I have sinned: for I have transgressed the commandment of the Lord, 
    and thy words: because I feared the people, and obeyed their voice.

Then today in church we sang the closing hymn #243, Let Us All Press On, 
"but the Lord, our Heavenly Father, him alone we will obey"

The voice of the Lord is the voice I want to listen to and obey as often as possible.

I felt the difference this week while playing the game of minutes. Inviting God to my thoughts more made me more receptive to His voice. 

I want to be a spiritual broadcaster for Christ.

One of Frank Laubach's ideas for winning the game of minutes is to pray for strangers as you pass by them. 

This thought came to mind several times this week as I played the game. 

I found myself feeling more loving toward friends and strangers. 

More willing to be understanding. 

More open to positive thoughts that God had for me, rather than my own.
