a boy with a smile so contagious.
a gift, he was God-sent and gracious. 
touched each of our lives
and the moment we arrive
in heaven he’ll be beaming to greet us. 

on this day we celebrate his legacy
to love with o’erflowing capacity. 
let us love without bounds, 
bc that’s how ‘Jansen’ sounds,
and mimic his Christ-like mentality.
