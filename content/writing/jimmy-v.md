*"If you laugh, you think, and you cry, that's a full day. That's a heck of a day. You do that seven days a week, you're going to have something special."*

*Jim Valvano*

--
When I first heard this quote from Jimmy V I was in my early teenage years. ESPN plays part of his speech before the ESPYs and this was one of the clips. I didn't get why *crying* was included in his definition of a full day. 

Crying is weird. 
Crying is for sadness. 
Crying is not fun. 

Why would I want to do that during my day? 

Things are different now...
