there is a version of me
that has already arrived —
I can almost see him,
calm in the way that only practice earns.

but here I am,
still getting the reps in,
still surprised by what the weight reveals
when I stop pretending it is lighter than it is.

I do not mind anymore.
the gap between who I am
and who I am becoming
is where I live.

and living here
turns out
to be enough.
