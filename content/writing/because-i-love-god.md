*Sunday thoughts*

I feel like its common to hear people say, “we go to church because we love god…”

This morning as I was getting ready for church my mind flashed to 10 years in the future when Remi might ask, 

“Why do we go to church?”

I thought about that situation and the response I might give. 

Reflecting on the idea that we go to church because we love God makes me think that as long as we show up, we are ok. 

No emphasis on what we do or how we do it. 

It made me think, I want my worship experience to be different. I want to go to church not because I love God, but to show love to God *AND* to show love to people. 

To me this makes church an active experience, not passive. I’m there because more often than not I receive love by showing it.
