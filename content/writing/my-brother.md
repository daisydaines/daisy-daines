have you ever met a kid who loved video games too much? 
he’d play for 10 hours straight and skip breakfast and lunch 
.
or how about a kid who blamed you for the window he shattered 
bc when he threw the batteries at my head they touched me last and hit the window afterward
.
or the kid who always won the competitions at school? 
be it business or sports, he even thought math was cool 
.
and what about the kid who always gets in your head 
to the point where you throw all his belongings out the window including his bed 
.
or the kid from high school who attracted all the girls? 
well, then we ain’t talkin about the same kid bc for him — girls was not plural 
.
but all jokes aside, this kid keeps us laughin
if you get to know him you’ll find his comedy aint lackin
.
and the more you get to know him the more loyal he’ll be
bc he doesn’t mess around when it comes to friends and family 
.
this kid’s passionate and dedicated — he does hard things for a living 
what makes him special is that he’s equally as giving. 
.
this kid looks out for others, he’s selfless and kind 
he’s always loved big — that hearts a gold mine 
.
it’s these traits that make him a man like no other 
and that’s why i’m proud to call this kid my brother 💛
