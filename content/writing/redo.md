reflecting on a strategy meeting that I just walked out of with Blakely, Cam Nelson, Aaron Baddley and Cam Walker. 

Blakely continues to impress me with his ability to think ahead, pivot and execute. 

He doesn't see the future, but as soon as he sees signals that things need to change he is making it happen.  

It feels so opposite of the Zartico way. Stick with what's working and keep it going. I think that is right and needs to happen at some level, but to grow and scale and get to the level that Redo wants to get to ($1B revenue) there is constant need for agility and reevaluating. 

At Zartico and even ISU and UVU it felt like there was fear in changing what was working. There was always hesitancy in trying something new. I get that and regularly feel that way. 

Redo is different. Business lines and ideas are being sold, designed and built and then quickly reevaluated. Even when they are going well. 

It's not perfect, but it is impressive. 

I've learned the best leaders are the best salesman. There is a new idea for the conversion cloud that the engineers are not stoked about. Cam mentioned the engineers not jumping on it and Blakely replied, "Well, you better get your pitch on." 

I've definitely been on the pitch side of "things that execs need done" and realize how good of a salesmen the best leaders are. I see this from Sterling and Eric constantly. How do we reengage and reignite the workforce day after day after day.
