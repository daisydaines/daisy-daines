do you see it? 

so casual. a small gesture compared to its powerful meaning. 

two. scissors. quote. victory. greeting. goodbye. fight on. 

peace. 

all of those, but mostly the last. 

do you feel it? 

so calm. a limited tangibility compared to its potent feeling.  

forgiveness. comfort. unity. order. harmony. rest. relationship. 

love. 

all of those, but mostly the last. 

because peace means a stronger connection. a sense of belonging to something more grand. to Someone more grand. 

that same Someone who calmed the raging sea helped another to walk on it. 

and that same Someone who comforted the roaring sinner caused lame to walk and take with them their comforter. 

and that same Someone who put to bed the doubts of a friend raises deceased friends from their slumber. 

so don’t forget that when the angels sang, “and on earth peace, goodwill toward men” 

they were only introducing the chorus, “because God so loved the world that He gave His Only Begotten Son.”

so let’s allow the Prince to declare peace on the windy, messy, and dead in our lives. after all it is His bread and butter… or maybe His bread and fishes 🙂
