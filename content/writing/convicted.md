across counties and states 
and on a regular basis 
robed in black with a gavel 
yet we hide our faces.

“swipe left” then “swipe right”
makin’ judgements too quickly. 
the accused has no defense
decisions made inadequately.

–

who made you the judge 
of who is and who is not?
it’s ok to have opinions
but those are your thoughts

“she’s hot, he’s cute 
he’s ugly, she’s dumb”
if we’re all to be teammates
why we playin’ one v. one?

–

the world’s quickly become 
“double tap” and “retweet” it.
we’re SO loved by our God, 
but scrollings left us depleted

of His care and affection 
that He shows through His Son
Jesus died a sinner’s death 
without commiting a single one. 

–

so i vote we let the judge
be the one wearing His robe
clothed in righteousness, perfection 
and sitting on His throne. 

Rests a crown atop His head
and another in His hand.
reaching He welcomes her,
him, and me into His promised land.
