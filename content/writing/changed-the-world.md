*A poem written for Grace. Mother's Day 2022*
______

on business in Chicago 
a stressed single mom of four,
trying to make a life for her kids, 
was fighting her own war

and then one day she met 
a soldier, in his own right, 
he had his battles, too
and he was putting up a fight 

it wasn’t long before the two
joined forces hand in hand 
though it likely wasn’t the life 
they had each originally planned

but Someone else had in mind
this union from the start 
He knew that it would happen
and He played a vital part

and now that things were going well
the six added number seven
the reason she’s called Grace, you ask?
because she’s divinely given 

and she had hopes and dreams 
to one day change the world
some encouraged and some laughed 
*“Oh, what a precious, little girl”*

and i can tell she sometimes thinks
her life hasn’t gone to plan 
but i would just remind her
the same happened to that man

and woman in that windy city
several decades in the past 
it seems to be their family story 
from the first page to the last 

and even though that story 
isn’t exactly as she wrote it out 
maybe now it’s better 
because the Author’s the editor now

so on those days when all is bleak,
with clouds of shame and regret,
she probably thinks back to her dreams 
how could she forget 

of how she wished she could have made 
a splash with her degree 
or had a brilliant idea
that changed the course of history 

but those aren’t the only ways
to change the world for good
in just a few words
i’d share this with her if i could 

you’ve changed my life for good
and that is hardly the start
of this life that we began
that day you stole my heart 

and if it weren’t for you we wouldn’t have
*our precious, little girl*
that is why, in my eyes,
you’ve already changed the world 💛
