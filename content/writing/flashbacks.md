apr 26
responding to remi at 10pm in a cold sweat and tears after spiking a fever earlier today gave me flashback to esther years ago today. being in remi’s room was hard for me. i had to walk out twice while grace held her. a handful of thoughts that competed with each other battled in my mind, “you didn’t do this for esther” vs “take care of remi”. or, “remember all the times you’ve done this before with esther” vs “this is the first time we’ve experienced this with remi” 

yesterday when i gave remi a blessing i closed my eye, took a deep breath, a almost started with “Esther G Daines”. i realized in that moment that any time I begin a priesthood blessing, Esther’s name comes to mind first. a tender reminder of the free blessings, but also power beyond this mortal life.
